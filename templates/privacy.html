Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Your privacy matters to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information Spendly collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using Spendly, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Data We Collect

We collect only what we need to run the Service:

  • Account information — your name, email address and a securely hashed password.
  • Expense data — the amounts, categories, dates and descriptions you choose to record.
  • Basic technical data — such as browser type and access times, used to keep the Service secure and working.

We do not collect bank account details, card numbers or any payment credentials.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your information is used to:

  • Create and manage your account.
  • Store your expenses and show you summaries and breakdowns.
  • Keep the Service secure and prevent misuse.
  • Contact you about important changes to the Service or this policy.

We do not sell your personal or financial data, and we do not use it for advertising.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored on secured servers and protected using reasonable technical and organisational measures, including password hashing and restricted access. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we work to protect your information.

We keep your data for as long as your account is active. You can edit or delete individual expenses at any time, and if you delete your account, your associated data will be removed.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly may rely on a small number of trusted providers, such as hosting services, to operate. These providers only process data on our behalf and are not permitted to use it for their own purposes.

Our pages load fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive basic technical information such as your IP address. We do not share your expense data with any third party unless required by law.

5. Your Rights

You may access, correct or delete your personal data at any time. You can also ask us for a copy of the data we hold about you.

6. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to the Spendly team. We will respond as soon as we can.
